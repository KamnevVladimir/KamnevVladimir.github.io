Privacy Policy

Expiry Keeper: Documents is designed to keep your document information private and local to your device.

Data collection

We do not collect, sell, rent, share, upload, or track your personal data. The app does not use analytics, advertising SDKs, third-party tracking, accounts, or a backend server.

Data stored on your device

Document names, document types, expiry dates, reminder settings, and renewal notes are stored locally on your device. We cannot access this data.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app uses local notifications only for expiry reminders you create. Notification permission can be changed in iOS Settings.

Support emails

If you contact support by email, we receive only the information you choose to send, such as your email address, message, and optional diagnostic details.

Children

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact kamnev.apps@gmail.com.
