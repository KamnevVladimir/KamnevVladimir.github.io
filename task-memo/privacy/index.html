Privacy Policy

Task Memo: Voice Notes is designed to keep your notes private and local to your device.

Data collection

We do not collect, sell, rent, share, upload, or track your personal data. The app does not use analytics, advertising SDKs, third-party tracking, accounts, or a backend server.

Data stored on your device

Voice notes, audio files, transcripts, and tasks are stored locally on your device. We cannot access this data.

Microphone and speech recognition

Microphone access is used only when you start recording. Speech recognition uses Apple system speech services to create editable text from your recording.

Support emails

If you contact support by email, we receive only the information you choose to send. Diagnostic details may include app version, iOS version, locale, and recent non-content app events. Your note text and audio are not attached automatically.

Children

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact kamnev.apps@gmail.com.
