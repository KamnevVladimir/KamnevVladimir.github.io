Privacy Policy

Task Memo: Voice Tasks is designed to keep your notes and tasks private and local to your device.

Data collection

Task Memo uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand app usage, improve reliability, and diagnose crashes. These services may collect product interaction data, app launch and feature usage events, device and app diagnostics, and crash reports.

We do not use advertising identifiers and do not use this data to track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

Data stored on your device

Voice notes, audio files, transcripts, and tasks are stored locally on your device. We cannot access this data, and Task Memo does not send note text, audio, transcripts, tasks, or reminders to Firebase.

App Store privacy summary

For App Store privacy labels, Task Memo may collect diagnostics and product interaction or usage analytics through Firebase. Tracking is not used. User content, note text, audio, transcripts, reminders, contact information, location, photos, and identifiers entered into the app are not collected by Task Memo.

Microphone and speech recognition

Microphone access is used only when you start recording. Speech recognition uses Apple system speech services to create editable text from your recording.

Notifications

Notifications are optional and are used only for task reminders that you create. Reminder notifications are scheduled locally on your device.

Support emails

If you contact support by email, we receive only the information you choose to send. Diagnostic details may include app version, iOS version, locale, and recent non-content app events. Your note text and audio are not attached automatically.

Children

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
