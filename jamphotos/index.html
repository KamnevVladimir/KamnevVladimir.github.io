What JamPhotos does on iPhone.

JamPhotos focuses on the jobs that usually force people to jump between several photo apps: selecting a set, making the images feel consistent, adding context, and exporting finished files quickly.

1. Select a set

Start with travel photos, marketplace images, product shots, social posts, or a personal album batch.

2. Shape the story

Combine images into collages, add captions, place text layers, apply frames, and crop the composition.

3. Match the look

Use light, color, and preset tools to make the photo set feel consistent before sharing.

4. Export locally

Save or send share-ready images from your iPhone without a cloud workspace or account-first flow.

JamPhotos FAQ

What is JamPhotos used for?

JamPhotos is for editing batches of iPhone photos with collages, captions, frames, presets, crop, light and color matching, then exporting share-ready images.

Does JamPhotos upload my photos?

No. Selected photos, edits, previews, text layers, collages, and export settings are processed locally on your iPhone.

Can JamPhotos edit a whole photo set?

Yes. JamPhotos is designed for sets, so you can apply a consistent look across a batch and adjust individual images when needed.
