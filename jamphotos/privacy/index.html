Privacy Policy

JamPhotos is designed as a local-first photo utility. Your photos and edits stay on your device unless you choose to export or share them.

Data collection

We do not collect, sell, rent, share, upload, or track your personal data. The app does not use analytics, advertising SDKs, third-party tracking, accounts, or a backend server.

Photos and edits

Selected photos, preview images, crop settings, presets, frames, text, and export settings are processed locally on your device. We cannot access your photos.

Photo Library access

Photo Library access is used only when you select photos to edit or save exported images. JamPhotos does not scan your library in the background.

Sharing and export

When you tap Send, iOS shows the system share sheet. The destination app or service you choose handles the exported files under its own policies.

Support emails

If you contact support by email, we receive only the information you choose to send. Diagnostic details may include app version, iOS version, locale, and recent non-content app events. Photos are not attached automatically.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact kamnev.apps@gmail.com.
