Subscriptions

JamPhotos Pro subscriptions are managed by Apple. Payment is charged to your Apple ID. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.

If a free trial is offered, it is shown on the App Store purchase sheet before confirmation. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple ID subscription settings.
