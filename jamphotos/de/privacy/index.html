Datenschutzerklärung

JamPhotos hilft dir, Stapel oder einzelne Fotos zu bearbeiten, Licht und Farbe anzugleichen, Collagen zu erstellen, Text und Rahmen hinzuzufügen und fertige Bilder zu exportieren. Deine Fotos bleiben auf deinem Gerät.

Data collection

We do not sell, rent, share, upload, or track your photos or photo content. JamPhotos uses Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics for basic app usage and crash diagnostics, and Adapty for subscriptions and purchase status. These services do not receive your photos, edits, text layers, exports, or Photo Library content.

Photos and edits

Selected photos, preview images, crop settings, presets, frames, text, collages, and export settings are processed locally on your device. We cannot access your photos.

Photo Library access

Photo Library access is used only when you select photos to edit or save exported images. JamPhotos does not scan your library in the background.

Firebase diagnostics

Firebase may process technical information such as app version, device model, iOS version, language, crash traces, performance diagnostics, and non-content app events.

Adapty subscriptions

Adapty may process subscription status, product identifiers, purchase events, anonymous user identifiers, device and app information needed to unlock Pro access and analyze subscription performance. Photo content is not uploaded to Adapty.

Sharing and export

When you tap Send, iOS shows the system share sheet. The destination app or service you choose handles the exported files under its own policies.

Support emails

If you contact support by email, we receive only the information you choose to send. Photos are not attached automatically.
