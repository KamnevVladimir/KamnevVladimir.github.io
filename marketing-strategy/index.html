Public strategy

Kamnev Apps marketing strategy.

Kamnev Apps is an independent native iPhone app label founded by Vladimir Kamnev. The strategy is intentionally practical: build small tools that solve one everyday job clearly, avoid bloated workflows, and earn discovery through App Store intent, useful content, and direct community context.

Ideal customer profile

Primary user

iPhone owner, 25-45, self-employed, freelancer, or knowledge worker. They manage their own documents, files, photos, and daily workflows without IT support. They value time, dislike bloated subscription apps, and prefer local processing for personal data.

Behavioral signals

  • Searches the App Store for single-purpose tools.
  • Leaves negative reviews on complex apps with too many features.
  • Prefers on-device processing and privacy-minded defaults.
  • Is open to fair, simple pricing instead of endless app bloat.

Secondary user

Small business owner or solopreneur who needs lightweight utility apps for daily admin: tracking license renewals, compressing files before sending, cleaning photo metadata, or capturing quick voice tasks.

Positioning

For iPhone users overwhelmed by bloated, subscription-heavy apps, Kamnev Apps offers focused native iOS utilities that each solve one job cleanly, with privacy-minded defaults and no unnecessary platform layer.

Channel priorities

1. App Store Optimization

Highest-intent discovery channel for utility apps. Priorities: keyword research, sharper subtitles, and screenshots that show the core workflow in three steps.

2. Reddit and niche communities

Use authentic participation in productivity, iPhone, privacy, and indie app communities. Share apps only when they clearly solve the problem being discussed.

3. X and Mastodon

Post short build updates, privacy-angle notes, and practical app tips. Engage with indie iOS developer and productivity audiences.

4. Product Hunt

Use one focused launch at a time, with the strongest hook and clean gallery assets.

5. Content and SEO

Publish simple use-case pages and posts tied to real search intent, such as tracking passport expiry on iPhone or editing photo batches locally.

30-day roadmap

Week 1 - Foundation

Audit App Store listings, improve subtitles, add direct App Store links, and set a simple App Store Connect baseline.

Week 2 - ASO and visuals

Refresh screenshots, add keyword-rich product descriptions, and publish a privacy-angle post.

Week 3 - Community seeding

Find relevant discussions, contribute useful context, submit apps to indie directories, and prepare one Product Hunt launch.

Week 4 - Launch and review push

Launch one app, ask early users for honest reviews, share a build story, and compare conversion signals against the baseline.
