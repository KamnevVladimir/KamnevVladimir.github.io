Service

SendShrink is a local photo compression utility. It creates smaller copies of selected photos for sharing, saving, messaging, email, uploads, and similar personal workflows.

Local processing

Compression runs on device. You choose which photos to process, save, or share. Original photos are not modified by the app.

Compression results

Output size, image quality, and visual detail depend on the selected preset, original photo, device processing, and system media frameworks. SendShrink does not promise an exact byte-perfect target size.
