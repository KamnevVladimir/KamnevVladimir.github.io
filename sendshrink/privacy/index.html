SendShrink Privacy Policy

SendShrink compresses selected photos locally on your device. The app does not require an account, backend upload, cloud sync, advertising or AI processing.

Original media is not modified. Compressed copies are saved to Photos only when you choose Save.

The app may use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics for app usage, reliability and crash diagnostics. Collected diagnostics may include product interaction, app launch and feature usage events, device/app diagnostics, performance information and crash reports. Selected photos, compressed photos, filenames, EXIF data and share destinations are not sent to Firebase by SendShrink.

Purchases and subscription status are processed by Apple and Adapty. Adapty is used to show purchase options, process purchases, restore purchases and understand subscription status. SendShrink does not use advertising identifiers and does not use data for tracking.

For App Store privacy labels, SendShrink may collect product interaction, diagnostics and purchase-related data through Firebase, Apple and Adapty. User photo content is processed on device and is not collected by SendShrink.
