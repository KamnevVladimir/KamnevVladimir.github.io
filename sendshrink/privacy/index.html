Privacy Policy

SendShrink compresses selected videos and photos locally on your device. The app does not require an account, backend upload, cloud sync, or AI processing.

Media content

Original videos and photos are not modified. Compressed copies are saved to Photos only when you choose Save. Media content is not uploaded to our servers.

Photo Library access

Photo Library access is used only when you choose media or save compressed results. SendShrink does not scan your library in the background.

Firebase diagnostics

Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics may process app usage, reliability, and crash diagnostics such as app launch events, feature usage, app version, device model, iOS version, language, crash traces, and diagnostics. User media content is not sent to Firebase.

Adapty purchases

Purchases and subscription status are processed by Apple and Adapty. Adapty may process product identifiers, purchase events, subscription status, anonymous user identifiers, and device/app information needed to unlock Pro access.

No tracking

SendShrink does not use advertising identifiers and does not use collected data for tracking.
