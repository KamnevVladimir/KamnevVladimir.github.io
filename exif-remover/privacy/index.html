Privacy Policy

EXIF Remover: Photo Metadata is a local-first privacy utility for iPhone.

Photo Processing

The app processes selected photos on device. Photo files, filenames, GPS data, camera data, and cleaned output are not uploaded to a server.

Original Photos

Original photos are never overwritten. When you save or share, EXIF Remover creates cleaned copies.

Accounts, Cloud, AI

The app does not require an account, does not use cloud sync, and does not use AI/API processing.

Purchases

Adapty is used to manage in-app purchases, paywall configuration, purchase status, and restore purchases. EXIF Remover does not send photo content or photo metadata to Adapty.
