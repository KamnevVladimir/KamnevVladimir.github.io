Privacy Policy

Metadata Remover checks and removes file metadata on your device. Purchases, diagnostics and optional creator attribution also use remote services.

Photo and video processing

Metadata Remover: EXIF GPS checks and removes file metadata on your device. It creates cleaned copies and leaves the originals unchanged. The app does not upload your photos, videos, filenames, or the metadata values it reads to Firebase, Adapty, GoMarketMe or Insert Affiliate.

Temporary files and sharing

The app uses temporary files on your device while importing, cleaning and preparing media for sharing. You choose the destination in the iOS share sheet. The receiving app or service handles the files or text you share. Removing metadata does not hide faces, addresses or other details visible in an image.

Verification report

The separate Share verification report action shares a text summary of the categories checked before and after cleaning, the fidelity result and any verification limitation. That summary contains no photo, filename or metadata values. An unavailable check remains marked as unavailable.

Diagnostics and purchases

We use Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Performance Monitoring for app usage, crash diagnostics and performance. These services can process app interaction events, app and device information, service identifiers and diagnostics. Adapty handles purchase status, paywalls and restoring Pro access using purchase-related identifiers and transaction information. These services involve remote processing even though media cleaning takes place on your device.

Creator attribution

With your permission, we use GoMarketMe and Insert Affiliate to associate a creator's referral link with an app installation and purchases, calculate commissions and measure our promotion. This can involve app and device identifiers, referral activity, device and network information, and purchase or subscription events. The partners' privacy policies also describe approximate location derived from an IP address. Subscription renewals may be processed on the server after the initial purchase.

Insert Links

We also use Insert Affiliate, with Adapty purchase events, to attribute creator referrals and calculate commissions. Insert Links sends device and operating-system details, screen characteristics, language and time-zone settings, network connection details and event timestamps to match a referral-link visit with an app installation. Insert Affiliate receives an installation-specific affiliate identifier and purchase-related data through the Adapty integration. Its privacy policy describes probabilistic matching and device signatures. Clipboard access is disabled.

Tracking permission and purchase servers

The app asks through Apple's tracking permission prompt before starting the optional affiliate SDKs. You can decline and continue cleaning, exporting and purchasing Pro. No creator code or app account is required. You can change tracking permission in iOS Settings. Revoking permission does not erase records already sent. Adapty continues to process purchases and restore access without tracking permission. With the Insert purchase integration configured, Adapty can forward purchase events, profile identifiers and custom user attributes to Insert servers even when no affiliate is assigned. This server flow is separate from the ATT-gated client SDKs. Revoking ATT does not itself stop server processing of earlier purchases or subscription renewals. Contact kamnev.apps@gmail.com about your data or a previous referral.

Service policies

Service policies: Firebase, Adapty, GoMarketMe.

Accounts, cloud and AI

The app does not require an account, does not use cloud sync for media processing, and does not use AI or API processing for metadata removal.

Support emails

If you contact support by email, we receive only the information you choose to send, such as your email address, message, and optional diagnostic details. Photos and videos are not attached automatically.
